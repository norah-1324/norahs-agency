Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This page explains what information the Growth Readiness Diagnostic collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Norah's Agency is a portfolio demonstration project, and the client examples shown in the diagnostic are fictional. Any information you enter is still handled exactly as described on this page.

Information We Collect

When you complete the diagnostic, we collect the information you enter:

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your company name, industry and website address
  • Your answers to the diagnostic questions

We do not ask for payment details, and the diagnostic does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How We Use It

  • To prepare your diagnostic results
  • To email your results to the address you provide
  • To send one follow-up email, which may include a brief review of the public pages of the website you entered
  • To respond if you reply or book a call

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with other companies for their own marketing.

Where It Is Stored and Who Helps Us

Your information is saved in a private spreadsheet that only Norah's Agency can open. To run the diagnostic, we rely on the following services:

  • Google Sheets, to store your submission
  • Gmail, to send our emails from norahs.agency@gmail.com
  • Google's website testing service, which receives only your website address in order to review its public pages
  • Google's Gemini service, which helps word the website review. It receives the review findings and your industry, and does not receive your name, email address or phone number
  • Cloudflare, which runs the diagnostic behind the scenes, and GitHub, which hosts this page
  • Cal.com, if you choose to book a call. Information you enter there is handled under Cal.com's own privacy policy

Our Use of Google Services

Norah's Agency uses its own Google account to send emails and store submissions. The diagnostic does not ask you to sign in with Google and does not access your Google account or any of your Google data.

Your Results Link

The results link in our emails contains your company name and your four diagnostic results. It does not contain your name, email address or phone number.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your submission until you ask us to delete it.

Your Choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing norahs.agency@gmail.com.

Contact

Questions or inquiries? Reach out to norahs.agency@gmail.com
